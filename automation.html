Automation and integrations

Connecting ServiceNow and executive channels to the incident record.

One incident record, several audiences

At Al Rajhi Bank, P1 status messages reached executives by SMS through a ServiceNow integration. Leaders could see a live update while the command center kept the incident record in one place.

Trigger awareness at declaration

At Marqeta, automated notifications alerted the executive channel when a major incident was declared. The initial alert handled awareness. A scheduled update then gave leadership the customer impact, recovery estimate and any decision that needed attention.
