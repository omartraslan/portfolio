Who owns the next decision?

At Al Rajhi Bank, I brought infrastructure, core banking, online banking and mainframe teams together with senior directors for payroll hypercare. A daily executive standup gave the CTO a place to resolve resource conflicts across departments.

Training and mentoring

At Marqeta, 384 engineers received incident management training, with 79% completion in one quarter. My approach to mentoring starts with a practical question: if you take over the bridge now, can you explain the impact, name the decision owner and say when the next update is due?
