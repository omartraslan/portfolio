How customer impact shapes severity, outreach and the next update.

Start with what the customer experiences

A technical alert tells me where to look. To write a customer update, I need to know which customers are affected, what their end users cannot do and when we will know more. At Al Rajhi Bank, I introduced a severity matrix based on business impact and urgency.
