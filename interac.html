First 90 days at Interac

A proposed plan to learn the environment, test the handoffs and agree on measures.

Days 1–30 · Learn the operating model

I would shadow on-call and customer communications teams, review recent incidents with responders and trace one payment disruption from detection to customer update. My first deliverable would be a map of decision owners, dependencies and escalation paths, validated by the people doing the work.

Days 31–60 · Test one handoff

I would choose a recurring friction point with the team and run a tabletop exercise around it. I would capture who declares severity, who approves external wording and how the next shift takes command. The output would be one revised handoff or update template, tested by responders.

Days 61–90 · Pilot and evaluate

I would pilot the revised handoff on an agreed service, compare time to first customer update and overdue review actions against the initial baseline, and gather feedback from the on-call team. I would bring leadership a recommendation to adopt, adjust or retire the pilot.
